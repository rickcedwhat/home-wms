High-Speed Middleware & Request Pipeline

Acts as an enterprise buffer between thousands of rugged scanner scans and NetSuite cloud ERP, preventing 429 rate limit errors.

server/routes/scanner.js Latency <5ms Error Normalization
2

Token-Based Authentication (RFC 5849 OAuth 1.0a)

Generates HMAC-SHA256 signatures with nonce, timestamp, and consumer/token secrets for NetSuite RESTlets, eliminating stored passwords and preventing replay attacks.

server/services/auth.js OAuth 1.0a HMAC-SHA256
3

Industrial GS1-128 & Barcode Parser

Parses composite warehouse barcodes containing Application Identifiers (AI 01 GTIN, AI 10 Lot, AI 30 Quantity) alongside standard UPC-A and Bin barcodes in a single scan.

server/services/barcodeParser.js GS1-128 AI UPC-A Bins
4

In-Memory Caching & Surgical Invalidation

Provides instant sub-5ms responses on repeated scans. When an inventory transfer occurs, only the affected item and bins are invalidated—leaving the rest of the cache warm.

server/services/cache.js Sub-5ms TTL Surgical Invalidation
5

Async Keyed Mutex (Race Condition Defense)

Locks simultaneous warehouse scans at the granular `item:bin` resource level, preventing double-pick race conditions and negative inventory while allowing parallel work on other bins.

server/services/concurrencyManager.js Promise Chaining FIFO Lock Queue
6

SuiteScript 2.1 RESTlet & Dynamic Sublists

Simulates NetSuite's official AMD architecture (define(['N/record', 'N/search'])). Implements dynamic mode sublist operations (selectNewLine, setCurrentSublistValue, commitLine, save) over SQLite.

server/netsuite-mock/restlet.js N/record Dynamic Sublists
